Project: EURO.G Systems — REDESIGN CONCEPT. Real client: PVC & aluminium
windows, doors, facades and glass systems, manufactured in Armenia.
Current site: https://eurog.lovable.app  (label the new one "Redesign concept")

GOAL
A portfolio piece that shows our full capabilities and gives a real wow
effect, and that would genuinely win EURO.G more and better leads. It must
look and work NOTHING like our previous showcase (Ararat Heights: dark,
cinematic, serif, residential sales). This one is an engineering brand.

STEP 1: RESEARCH FIRST (short report before designing)
Study the best sites in this industry (e.g. Schüco, Reynaers, Internorm,
VEKA, Velux) and what Armenian buyers search for. Tell me: what the best
do, what is missing in Armenia, and how EURO.G can stand out.

DESIGN DIRECTION: "PRECISION ENGINEERING"
- Bright, airy, technical: white, aluminium greys, a cool glass-blue tint,
  EURO.G's own brand colour as the accent (take logo/colours from the
  current site).
- Visual language of technical drawings: dimension lines, section cuts,
  exploded views, spec labels in a mono font; a modern grotesk for
  headlines. Precise grid, generous white space.
- Imagery: architectural photography (modern Armenian homes, offices,
  facades with big glazing), macro shots of profiles, seals, hardware.
  Use good real photos from the current site; generate the rest in one
  consistent, crisp, daylight style.

SIGNATURE WOW MOMENT
A 3D window profile in the hero that the visitor can rotate. On scroll it
explodes into its layers (frame, chambers, reinforcement, seals, glass
units) with labels. A switch turns on "thermal view": an infrared heat
map shows cold leaking through an old window versus warmth staying inside
EURO.G's.

FEATURES (each must be useful to the business, not decoration)
- Window configurator: choose the product type, opening, size and profile
  system, then see the cross-section, U-value/thermal rating, sound
  insulation and a price estimate in AMD → "Send this configuration for an
  exact quote" (the sales team gets a precise, qualified lead)
- Energy & noise calculator: current windows vs EURO.G → yearly heating
  savings in AMD and dB noise reduction
- Production line that animates as you scroll: profile cutting, welding,
  hardware, glazing, quality control (proves it's manufactured, not resold)
- Real projects with before/after photo sliders, filterable by building
  type and city
- Paths for each audience: homeowners (simple guide), architects
  (technical sheets, CAD downloads), developers (volume, timelines),
  dealers (partner programme + application form)
- Smart quote flow: configuration or photo/drawing upload → measurement
  visit booking with a calendar → confirmation
- HY / RU / EN written natively; mobile-first; fast; SEO + structured data

WOW STANDARD
Amaze within 5 seconds and keep surprising while scrolling. Award-level
quality (Awwwards "Site of the Day"), not a template or a generic AI site.
Smooth micro-interactions on every control. Smooth on phones too. Before
showing me anything, critique it honestly as a senior creative director
("what's generic here?") and fix it first.

PROCESS
1) Research report  2) Concept, brand look and structure + 2–3 directions
for the opening section → I approve  3) Build the live demo  4) Walkthrough
video, screenshots of every feature, case study (Before → After with the
current site)  5) Add to both decks and the portfolio page.
